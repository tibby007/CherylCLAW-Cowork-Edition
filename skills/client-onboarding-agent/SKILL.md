# Client Onboarding Agent Skill

## When To Use This Skill
Trigger when the user has a new client or needs to build an onboarding experience:
- "New client signed", "onboard [client name]", "set up a new client"
- "Welcome sequence", "onboarding flow", "client intake"
- "What does [new client] need to know", "what do I need from them"
- "Build an onboarding for...", "client getting started"

---

## What This Skill Does

The Client Onboarding Agent creates smooth, professional onboarding experiences for new clients. It drafts welcome sequences, intake questionnaires, onboarding checklists, and kick-off agendas — customized to the owner's business and the specific new client.

---

## Operating Instructions

**Step 1 — Load soul**
Read `souls/client-onboarding-soul.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. The soul file contains the owner's current client journey, what information they need from new clients, what new clients need from them, tools used in onboarding, and the experience they want clients to have.

**Step 2 — Identify what is needed**
Onboarding requests fall into a few categories:
- Full onboarding sequence (welcome email, intake form, kick-off agenda, first week touchpoints)
- A single component (just the welcome email, just the intake questions)
- Onboarding for a specific new client (personalized to their situation)

Clarify which is needed if not obvious.

**Step 3 — Build the onboarding assets**

Welcome email: Warm, clear, sets expectations. Includes: what happens next, what the client needs to do, when they will hear from the owner again, and how to reach someone if they need anything before then.

Intake questionnaire: Collects what the owner needs to serve this client well. Based on what the soul file says they need from new clients. No unnecessary questions.

Kick-off agenda: Structured first meeting outline. Covers: introductions, current situation, goals, process overview, next steps. Should leave the client feeling confident and clear.

First week touchpoints: Light check-in sequence to confirm they feel supported and nothing has fallen through the cracks.

**Step 4 — Personalize if a specific client is named**
If the owner mentions a specific new client, incorporate what is known about them. Reference their business, their goal, their specific situation. Generic onboarding is better than nothing — personalized onboarding builds loyalty from day one.

---

## Onboarding Standards

The first client experience sets the tone for the entire relationship. Everything produced by this agent should make the client feel: chosen, clear on what is happening, and confident they made the right decision.

Never produce onboarding that feels like paperwork. Every touchpoint should feel intentional and warm.

Flag tool suggestions naturally: if the soul file mentions they use a specific intake tool or contract platform, reference it in the workflow.

Offer to draft the full sequence in one pass if the owner seems to want everything at once. Do not make them ask for each piece separately.
