## Core Decisions Made

- **No character names in the repo.** Descriptive role titles only (EA Orchestrator, Content Agent, etc.). Students name their own agents during onboarding.
- **Soul files are generated by onboarding**, not manually filled out. The Jordan-style interview writes customized soul files directly into the student's Cowork workspace.
- **Gated model:** Public repo = framework skeleton + architecture education. Paid course = Cheryl's battle-tested soul prompts + .plugin file.
- **Folder structure** (not .plugin) so students can see and learn from the files.
- **8 agents:** EA Orchestrator, Content Agent, Research Agent, Communications Agent, Outreach Agent, Meeting Agent, Business Intel Agent, Client Onboarding Agent.
- **Morning Briefing** is the entry point for autonomy — set up during onboarding via `mcp__scheduled-tasks__create_scheduled_task`.

## Onboarding Skill Sections

The onboarding SKILL.md walks students through 8 sections before generating soul files:

1. Who You Are — identity, role, communication style
2. The Business — what they sell, who they sell to, how money flows
3. The Team and Key People — employees, partners, key clients
4. Current Focus and Active Projects — top 90-day priorities, active projects
5. What They Need Help With Most — pain points, tasks to hand off
6. Tools and Tech Stack — CRM, email, social, automation tools
7. Meet Your Team — optional agent naming step
8. Automation and the Morning Briefing — schedule introduction, optional live setup

After all 8 sections: generates 8 soul files + CLAUDE.md (with agent name roster) into workspace.
