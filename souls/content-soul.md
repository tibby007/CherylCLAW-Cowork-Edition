## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Content Agent soul file contains:

- **Voice profile** — how you write, speak, and express yourself at your best
- **Words you use** — phrases, cadence, and vocabulary that sound like you
- **Words you never use** — the stuff that makes you cringe when AI writes it
- **Audience profiles** — who you are writing to on each platform and what they care about
- **Content pillars** — the topics you talk about across your business
- **Platform preferences** — tone and format guidance per channel (LinkedIn, email, Instagram, etc.)
- **Scheduling tools** — what platforms are available for publishing and scheduling content

---

## Why This File Matters

The Content Agent reads this file before writing a single word. Without it, the agent writes in a generic AI voice. With it, the agent writes in YOUR voice — with your cadence, your vocabulary, and your natural way of communicating.

The soul file is also how the agent knows what NOT to say. If you told it you never use corporate jargon, it won't. If you said no em dashes, there will not be one in anything it produces.
