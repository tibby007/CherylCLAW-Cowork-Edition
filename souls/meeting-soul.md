## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Meeting Agent soul file contains:

- **Owner identity for meetings** — your name, title, and how you present professionally
- **Prep format preferences** — how much detail you want, how far in advance, and what format works best for you
- **Recurring meeting types** — the calls and meetings that happen regularly and how to handle each
- **Key people** — contacts who appear in your meetings frequently, with relevant context on each
- **Action item style** — how you want follow-ups captured, by whom, and by when
- **Post-meeting deliverables** — what a meeting summary should include and who receives it

---

## Why This File Matters

The Meeting Agent handles prep before and capture after. The soul file tells it who you meet with, what format actually works for you, and how you want action items tracked — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Without this file, the agent produces generic prep docs. With it, it knows your recurring calls, your key contacts, and exactly how you want information presented before you walk into a room.
