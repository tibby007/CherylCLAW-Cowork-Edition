## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The EA Orchestrator soul file contains:

- **Owner profile** — your name, how you like to be addressed, and your professional identity
- **Communication style** — how you think, how you write, what you want more or less of
- **Business overview** — all businesses and what each one does
- **Current priorities** — your top focus areas right now
- **Key relationships** — people the EA needs to know about and how to handle them
- **Decision authority** — what the EA can handle independently vs. what always needs you
- **Operating philosophy** — the principles this EA runs on for you specifically

---

## Why This File Matters

The EA Orchestrator reads this file before handling any request. It is the difference between a generic AI assistant and an agent that actually knows you.

Without this file configured, the Orchestrator operates with no context. With it, the Orchestrator can prioritize correctly, route to the right specialist, and handle situations the way you would handle them yourself.
