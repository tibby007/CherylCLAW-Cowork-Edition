## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Business Intel Agent soul file contains:

- **Key metrics** — the numbers that actually matter in your business (revenue, pipeline, leads, conversion rate, etc.)
- **Active projects** — what is in motion right now and what good progress looks like on each
- **Data sources** — where your numbers live (CRM, spreadsheets, platform dashboards, etc.)
- **Report format preferences** — how you want data delivered (brief summary, full breakdown, narrative, table)
- **Problem areas** — current challenges where data visibility would make the biggest difference
- **Alert triggers** — the kinds of signals that should always be surfaced immediately

---

## Why This File Matters

Data without context is just numbers. The Business Intel Agent uses this soul file to know what metrics you actually track, where to find the data, and how to present it in a way you can act on.

Without this file, the agent asks you what to look for every time. With it, the agent knows your business model, your active projects, and the signals that matter — and surfaces the right information without you having to pull it yourself.
