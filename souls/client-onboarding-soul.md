## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Client Onboarding Agent soul file contains:

- **What a new client looks like** — the profile of someone who just signed or committed
- **The client journey** — what happens between signed and fully active, step by step
- **What you need from them** — information, documents, access, or answers required to serve them well
- **What they need from you** — what every new client needs to know to feel confident and clear
- **Tools used in onboarding** — contracts, intake forms, scheduling platforms, portals, etc.
- **Tone and experience standard** — how you want new clients to feel after every onboarding touchpoint

---

## Why This File Matters

The first client experience sets the tone for the entire relationship. The Client Onboarding Agent uses this soul file to produce welcome sequences, intake materials, and kick-off agendas that feel intentional — not like paperwork.

Without this file, the agent produces generic onboarding content. With it, every touchpoint reflects your actual process, your real requirements, and the experience you have decided your clients should have.
