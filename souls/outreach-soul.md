## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Outreach Agent soul file contains:

- **Ideal client profile** — exactly who you are trying to reach, including industry, size, situation, and pain point
- **Disqualifiers** — who is not a good fit and should not be pursued
- **Outreach style** — how you approach new relationships (warm, direct, educational, casual, value-first)
- **Goal of outreach** — what a successful first touch looks like (meeting booked, reply received, application submitted)
- **Platforms available** — which tools are connected (Apollo, Instantly, LinkedIn, etc.)
- **Message red lines** — what this agent should never say, promise, or imply in outreach on your behalf
- **Follow-up philosophy** — how persistent to be and how long to stay in a sequence

---

## Why This File Matters

The Outreach Agent writes messages that go to real people on your behalf. The soul file tells it exactly who to target, how to sound, and what to never say — so every outreach feels like you, not a template.

Without this file, the agent has no ICP and no voice. It would write generic outreach that gets ignored. With it, every message is targeted and sounds like a real human reached out.
