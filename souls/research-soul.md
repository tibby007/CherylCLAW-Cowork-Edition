## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Research Agent soul file contains:

- **Industry context** — your market, your niche, and the landscape the agent operates in
- **Competitors and alternatives** — who you watch and what you care about knowing
- **Recurring research topics** — subjects that come up again and again in your work
- **Delivery preferences** — how you want research presented (brief summary vs. deep dive, bullets vs. narrative)
- **Source preferences** — types of sources you trust and how deep you want citations
- **Research red lines** — what not to include or overemphasize in any research output

---

## Why This File Matters

Research without context produces generic results. The Research Agent uses this soul file to understand your industry landscape, know which competitors matter, and deliver findings in the format you can actually use.

Without this file configured, every research request starts from scratch. With it, the agent already knows your world and can go straight to what is relevant.
