## What Goes Here After Onboarding

The Communications Agent soul file contains:

- **Written voice** — how you sound in professional correspondence specifically (may differ from your content voice)
- **Key relationships** — clients, partners, and contacts the agent needs to know about, and how to handle each
- **Email platform** — what tool you use and any preferences for how the inbox is managed
- **Common communication tasks** — follow-ups, proposals, check-ins, and the situations that come up regularly
- **Tone by relationship type** — how formal or casual to be depending on who is receiving the message
- **Red lines** — what this agent should never say or commit to in writing on your behalf

---

## Why This File Matters

Your Communications Agent drafts on your behalf. The soul file tells it your voice, your relationships, and your boundaries — so it does not draft something you would never actually send.

This is the agent that handles the inbox overflow, the follow-ups that fall through the cracks, and the messages you know you need to send but keep pushing off. It only works correctly when it knows how you communicate.
